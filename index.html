Questions, answered

Quick answers before you start.

How do I download a TikTok video without watermark?

Copy the video link from TikTok, paste it in the box at the top of this page and press Download. Then choose Download HD video.

Can I download TikTok photo slideshows?

Yes. Paste the link of the slideshow (photo post) and you can save each photo individually, download all of them as one ZIP file, or save the post's audio as MP3.

Where do my downloads go?

To your browser's normal Downloads folder. On iPhone, open Safari's download list or the Files app. If a video opens in a new tab instead, press and hold it (or right-click) and choose Save.

Does it work on iPhone, Android and computers?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser — no app or extension needed.

Can I download private videos?

No. Only public posts can be downloaded. If a link fails, check that the post is public and still available.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is completely free and there is nothing to sign up for.

Is it okay to download videos?

Only download content you created or have permission to use, and always respect the creator's rights and credit. This site is not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.
